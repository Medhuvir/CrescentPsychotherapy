About Yanina

A space where you feel safe to be yourself

Yanina Mitelman is a New York licensed clinical social worker (LCSW) offering trauma-informed care for adults and couples. Her background blends somatic, DBT, and CBT approaches with quiet, grounded support.

Her work is rooted in presence, not pressure. Sessions feel warm, human, and paced around what feels most helpful for you—no jargon, no hurry.

Telehealth is available, and Yanina is accepting new clients who want practical tools, emotional safety, and a steady place to be seen.

Approaches I use in our work

Individual Counseling

One-on-one sessions focused on your unique story, coping tools, and meaningful change.

Couples Counseling

Building communication, trust, and understanding together in a gentle, practical way.

DBT + CBT

Skills for emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and shifting the thinking patterns that hold you back.

Somatic Experiencing

Releasing stored trauma through body awareness and nervous system support.
