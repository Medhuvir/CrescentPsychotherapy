Voice & tone

Brand personality

groundedwarmpresenthealinggentletrustworthynon-clinicalhuman

Writing principles

Speak to people, not patients. Use plain language. Avoid jargon unless paired with a plain explanation. Lead with empathy. Never pathologize. Short sentences over long ones.

Do say

"You deserve a space where you feel safe." · "We work at your pace." · "Book a free 15-minute call." · "I'm here when you're ready."

Avoid

"Treat your mental illness." · "Evidence-based protocols." · "Clinically proven outcomes." · Anything that sounds like a brochure or a hospital intake form.
